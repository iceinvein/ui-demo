## Design Context

### Users
Developers, designers, and potential employers/clients browsing a personal portfolio of hand-crafted React UI components. They're evaluating craft quality and attention to detail.

### Brand Personality
Refined, understated, craft-focused. The work should speak for itself.

### Aesthetic Direction
Minimal / gallery — content-forward, let the components be the hero. Subtle structure, generous whitespace, curated feel like a design gallery. Avoid generic developer portfolio templates.

### Design Principles
1. **Let the work breathe** — generous spacing, no visual clutter, components are the focus
2. **Create rhythm through variety** — break monotonous grids, vary section layouts, use spatial hierarchy
3. **Subtle structure** — hierarchy through spacing and typography weight, not decorative elements
4. **Intentional asymmetry** — avoid centering everything; left-aligned compositions feel more designed
5. **Craft signals craft** — every spacing choice, alignment, and proportion should feel deliberate
